# 13 — High-velocity self-serve funnel

Product-led growth funnels move in days. This log has deals converting from
signup to paid in under a week. The scenario highlights the velocity metric
(average days in stage) at the short end of the spectrum and shows the advance
rate still computes cleanly when cycles are tiny.

Run:

    dealflow forecast -p demos/13-fast-velocity/pipeline.yml -d demos/13-fast-velocity/deals.csv
